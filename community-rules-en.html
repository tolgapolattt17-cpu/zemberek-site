Community Rules

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Our Goal

The Zemberek Community is a space where genuine watch collectors share their watches in a respectful and safe environment. These rules apply to everyone.

Do

  • Share your own watch and genuine information; state the brand and model correctly.
  • Be respectful to others; comment constructively.
  • Share someone else's photo only with their permission.

Do Not

  • Harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats and discrimination.
  • Nudity, sexual content, violence or shocking imagery.
  • Presenting fake or misleading watches/products; passing off a replica as genuine.
  • Spam, advertising, repetitive content, off-platform sales / scam attempts.
  • Sharing others' personal data (phone, address, ID, payment details).
  • Content that infringes copyright or personality rights; content that promotes illegal activity.
  • Impersonation, using multiple accounts to evade rules, submitting fake membership proof.

Reporting and Blocking

If you see content or a member that breaks the rules, choose Report from the post's "…" menu; you can block a member who bothers you. We usually review reports within 24 hours and take the necessary action. For an urgent matter that must stay confidential, write to destek@zemberek.app.

Enforcement

Depending on the violation: content removal, a warning, temporary suspension or permanent account closure. In serious cases an account may be closed without warning. To appeal a decision, write to destek@zemberek.app.
